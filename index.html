Guide 01

How to begin freelancing

Freelancing means offering a service to clients instead of working as a permanent employee. Beginners should start small and focus on one clear service.

  • Choose one service: for example, basic HTML landing pages, data entry, document formatting or simple research.
  • Practise first: create two or three sample projects that demonstrate what you can do.
  • Write an honest profile: explain your skills and experience accurately. Do not claim expertise you do not have.
  • Check payment rules: confirm that the platform supports your country and a payment method you can use.

There is no guaranteed income. Finding clients takes time, practice and consistent communication.

Guide 02

How to search for remote jobs safely

Use established job boards and the employer's official careers page when possible. Read the full job description and confirm the company domain before sharing personal information.

  • Be cautious if a recruiter asks for an upfront fee, gift cards or cryptocurrency.
  • Do not share banking passwords, one-time codes or identity documents with unverified contacts.
  • Check the role's location, schedule, pay terms and eligibility requirements.
  • Remember that applying does not guarantee an interview or a job offer.

Important

Protect yourself from online earning scams

Warning signs

  • Guaranteed high income for little or no work.
  • Upfront fees to unlock a job or withdraw supposed earnings.
  • Pressure to act immediately or recruit friends.
  • Requests for passwords, OTPs or sensitive documents through unofficial channels.

Before you join a platform

  • Read its terms, privacy policy and payment conditions.
  • Search for independent reviews and complaints.
  • Check minimum payout, fees and country restrictions.
  • Start cautiously and never spend money you cannot afford to lose.
